A seat at our table

A little taste of the Caucasus, right here in Yerevan.

There is something wonderfully simple about khinkali: a soft fold of dough, a savory filling, and that first warm sip of broth. It is food made for sharing and for slowing down.

At Golden Khinkali, the menu brings that spirit together with other familiar Caucasian favorites. Come for dumplings, stay for another plate, and make the evening your own.
